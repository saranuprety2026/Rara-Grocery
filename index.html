The taste & aroma of South Asia, stocked fresh in Canberra's north.

Rara Grocery is your one-stop Nepalese & South Asian grocery store in Franklin, ACT. Whether you're looking for authentic spices from back home, your favourite brand of noodles, frozen momos ready to steam, or puja items for your next ceremony — we've got it all.

Our shelves are stocked with familiar brands and hard-to-find items sourced directly, so you never have to miss the flavours of home.
